Chikorita is a small, quadruped Grass‑type Pokémon that makes its home in grassland areas of the Johto region. A gentle sweet scent drifts from the leaf perched on its head, and the creature is known for its calm, friendly nature, often spending time basking in the sun’s warm rays while its leaf helps it sense humidity and temperature.

It develops into Bayleef and later into Meganium, traveling through its three‑stage evolutionary line. Chikorita’s unique abilities are leaf‑guard and overgrow, which complement its habit of using the leaf to check the surrounding environment. In battles it waves the leaf to keep opponents at bay, and the accompanying fragrance has a soothing effect, creating a cozy and welcoming atmosphere for both allies and foes.

This Pokémon can learn a wide range of moves, including leaf‑storm, vine‑whip, solar‑beam, grassy‑glide and many others listed in its move pool. With a capture rate of 45 and a non‑legendary, non‑mythical status, Chikorita is a common sight in its native grasslands, where it reliably uses its aromatic leaf to monitor the weather and enjoy sunny days.
