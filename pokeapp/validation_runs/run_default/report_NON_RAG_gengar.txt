Gengar, the evolved form of Haunter and the final stage of the Gastly line, is a spectral twin‑twin of shadow and mischief. From its humble origins as a drifting Gastly in the mist‑shrouded caves of Mt. Moon (Kanto) and the haunted corridors of the Pokémon Tower, it matures into a towering, grinning phantom that haunts abandoned structures, power plants, and fog‑laden forests across every region — from the icy cliffs of Unova’s Relic Castle to the neon‑lit streets of Kalos’s Snow Bell City. Trainers who cross its path are drawn by the faint, echoing cackle that seems to summon the very darkness it wields.

The creature’s most defining trait is its **Levitate** ability, granting it immunity to Ground‑type attacks and a floating presence that makes it feel both untouchable and ominous. Coupled with its hidden **Cursed Body** power — capable of disabling an opponent’s move with a single touch — Gengar becomes a master of psychological warfare. Its signature competitive toolkit centers on the iconic **Shadow Ball**, a move that not only embodies its dark aura but also pierces defenses with uncanny precision. Yet true Gengar flair is found in its chilling **Hypnosis**, the unsettling **Destiny Bond** that can turn a battle into a shared fate, and the volatile **Focus Blast**, a surge of raw spectral energy that rewards daring strategy.

In the battled‑rings of competitive play, Gengar is celebrated for its lightning‑fast speed and its uncanny ability to cripple opposing teams before they can react. Its ghost‑type moves weave a tapestry of fear and fortune, while its occasional foray into poison‑type attacks — like **Sludge Wave** — adds a corrosive edge that few can withstand. Whether perched atop a crumbling tower in Kanto, lurking in the mist of a Johto forest, or surfacing in the neon shadows of Alola’s Melemele, Gengar remains the quintessential haunter of nightmares, a Pokémon whose very existence blurs the line between illusion and reality.
