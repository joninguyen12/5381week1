Lucario, known as the “Aura Pokémon,” belongs to the genus *Aura Pokémon* and first emerged in the Sinnoh region. Its upright blue form can tap into the auras of all living things, allowing it to sense emotions, intent, and even thoughts from more than half a mile away. This rare sensitivity is highlighted throughout its Pokédex entries—whether it is “reading the auras of all things” in Diamond, “identifying and taking in the feelings of creatures over half a mile away” in Platinum, or “detecting the species and emotions of a being from a great distance” in Moon.

The legends of Lucario’s pack behavior add a mythic edge to its lore: it controls mysterious energy waves called auras, waves that can pulverize boulders and are strong enough to hunt prey collectively. Its reputation for fairness is underscored by the line that “only Trainers who have justice in their hearts can earn this Pokémon’s trust,” making it a companion for those who value honor and righteousness.

Lucario’s evolutionary line begins with the small, dog‑like Riolu, which matures into the majestic Lucario. While its natural habitat remains unspecified in the Pokémon world, its widespread presence across the Sinnoh region suggests a wide‑ranging, adaptable nature. As a Generation IV creation, Lucario continues to embody the harmonious blend of strong will and gentle empathy that defines the legends of the Pokémon world.
