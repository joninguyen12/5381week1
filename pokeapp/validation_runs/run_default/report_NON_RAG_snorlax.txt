**Snorlax‑line** – The hefty, lavender‑hued Snorlax is the solitary final form of a two‑stage family that begins with the rotund baby Munchlax.  Munchlax hatches from a fragile egg that only the most patient trainers can coax into a high‑friendship state; once that bond deepens, the child blossoms into the gargantuan Snorlax.  Unlike many Pokémon that undergo multiple metamorphoses, Snorlax remains a one‑step evolution, a design choice that underscores its mythic stature: a creature so massive that it needs no further transformation to prove its dominance.

In the wild, Snorlax first made its mark in the Kanto region, literally blocking the path on Routes 11 and 12 with a sleepy sprawl that forced adventurers to seek the legendary Poké Flute.  Since those early days, the species has turned up in a variety of locales — from the mist‑shrouded valleys of Johto to the sun‑baked plains of Hoenn, and later in the rolling fields of Galar and the towering peaks of the Alola archipelago.  Its presence is often accompanied by a distinctive, resonant snore that reverberates through caves and forests, warning travelers that a true heavyweight lies ahead.

Competitive battlers prize Snorlax for its towering HP stat and a toolbox that feels tailor‑made for endurance.  Signature moves such as **Curse** (which boosts its already formidable Attack and Defense at the cost of Speed) and **Belly Drum** (a single‑use, massive Attack boost that consumes half its HP) turn the Pokémon into a walking fortress.  Classic support moves like **Rest** paired with **Sleep Talk** let it recover while still threatening opponents, while **Body Slam** and **Earthquake** provide reliable STAB coverage.  Together, these abilities and moves cement Snorlax as the living embodiment of “quiet strength” — a gentle giant whose roar can reshape entire battlefields.
