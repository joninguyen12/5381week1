Snorlax (Pokédex number 143) is classified as a “Sleeping Pokémon” and is described as black and humanoid in shape, living in mountain regions.  According to the source data it can be caught with a capture rate of 25 and is not marked as a baby, legendary, or mythical species.  

It belongs to the monster egg group and follows a simple two‑stage evolution line: it first evolves from the baby‑stage Pokémon Munchlax and then reaches its final form, Snorlax.  

Across many game versions the Pokédex flavor text portrays Snorlax as an extremely lazy creature that spends most of its day eating and sleeping, can devour over 800 pounds of food, and never gets an upset stomach even when the food is moldy or rotten.  Several entries note that its stomach’s digestive juices can dissolve any poison and that its deep snores or rumblings are sometimes heard as its “cry.”  Children are said to use its expansive belly as a play area, and the descriptions differ slightly from version to version.
