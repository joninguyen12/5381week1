Totodile, known as the Big Jaw Pokémon, is a Water‑type creature that makes its home at water’s edge in the Johto region. Its well‑developed jaws are strong enough to crush almost anything, so even its trainer must stay careful around it.  

Despite its small and rugged appearance, Totodile will bite anything that moves without hesitation. Its bite can cause serious injury, and its rough nature makes it a spirited companion. The Pokémon’s abilities, sheer‑force and torrent, amplify this aggressive style.  

Over time Totodile evolves into croconaw and then feraligatr, growing into a more formidable Water‑type. It can learn a variety of moves — including bite, crunch, water gun and aqua jet — reflecting its versatile battle repertoire.
