Lapras is a gentle, blue‑colored Pokémon shaped like a fish that makes its home in the sea.  It is classified as a **Transport Pokémon**, belongs to the “monster” and “water1” egg groups, and is not a baby, legendary, or mythical creature.  

The Pokédex entries from many game versions describe Lapras as a caring creature that can ferry people (and sometimes other Pokémon) across the water on its back and will sing a pleasant cry when it’s in a good mood.  Some versions stress its high intelligence and ability to understand human speech, while others note that humans have hunted it almost to extinction, causing its numbers to dwindle.  The wording varies slightly between versions, reflecting the different flavor‑text lines provided for each release.  

Evolution‑wise, the data shows that Lapras has no pre‑evolutions or evolutions — its species list contains only “lapras” itself — so it stands alone in its family.  Its capture rate is 45, making it somewhat easier to encounter than many other rare Pokémon.
