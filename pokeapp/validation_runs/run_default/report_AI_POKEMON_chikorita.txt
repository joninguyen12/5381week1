Chikorita is a small, green – quadruped – Pokémon whose genus is literally “Leaf Pokémon.”  It makes its home in grassland areas and is known for the gentle, sweet scent that drifts from the leaf on its head.  The Pokédex repeatedly notes that this leaf helps it sense humidity and temperature, and that the creature loves to soak up sunshine.

The English flavor‑text for Chikorita appears in many game versions, and while each version phrases it a little differently, they all describe a docile creature that uses its leaf to steady foes and to create a calm, friendly atmosphere.  Some entries emphasize how the leaf checks the weather, others focus on the pleasant aroma and the habit of seeking warm spots; together they paint a picture of a gentle, sun‑loving Pokémon whose scent can soothe those nearby.

Chikorita’s evolutionary line proceeds in the order “chikorita → bayleef → meganium,” so it will eventually become Bayleef and then Meganium.  It is classified in the “monster” and “plant” egg groups, and the JSON records that it is neither a baby, legendary, nor mythical Pokémon.
