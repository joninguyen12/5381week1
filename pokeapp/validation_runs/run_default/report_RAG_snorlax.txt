Snorlax, numbered 143 in the Pokédex, is a black, humanoid Normal‑type Pokémon that comes from the mountainous areas of the Kanto region. It is not a baby, legendary, or mythical creature, and its egg group is classified as monster. With a capture rate of 25 it evolves from the smaller Munchlax before reaching its final form.

In the wild it spends most of its day eating and sleeping. Its appetite is enormous — it will keep feeding until it has consumed over 880 pounds of food, and only then does it finally rest. The creature’s massive stomach can break down any kind of nourishment, even moldy or rotten items, and its digestive juices are strong enough to dissolve poison. Its cry is more likely a snore or the rumble of a hungry belly than a typical Pokémon call.

Snorlax’s abilities include Gluttony, Immunity, and Thick‑Fat, and it can learn a very wide range of moves such as Rest, Snore, Earthquake, and Headbutt. Children are known to play on its expansive belly, and its habitat remains limited to mountainous locales within Kanto.
