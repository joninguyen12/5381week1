Lapras is a blue, fish‑shaped Pokémon known as the “Transport Pokémon.”  It lives in the sea and is classified as a Water / Ice type.  In the games it can be encountered with the abilities Hydration, Shell‑Armor or Water‑Absorb, and it is most famously recognized for carrying people and other Pokémon on its back across the water.  

Its high intelligence lets it understand human speech, and many entries in the flavor texts describe it as a gentle creature that will ferry travelers on its back and even sing a pleasant cry when it is in a good mood.  The Red and Yellow versions note that it was once “over‑hunted almost to extinction” yet still offers a comfortable ride, while the Ruby and Omega‑Ruby versions recall that it now sings plaintively as it searches for the few remaining members of its kind.  These descriptions are all drawn from the published flavor entries.  

Although Lapras belongs to the Kanto region, it is not tied to any specific evolutionary line—its species name stands alone.  Its move pool includes techniques such as Surf, Waterfall, Ice Beam, Hydro Pump and many others drawn from its official move list, and it is recorded with a capture rate of 45.  Today, spotting a Lapras in the wild is a rare treat, a reminder of a once‑abundant sea dweller that still carries the memories of the oceans it once roamed.
