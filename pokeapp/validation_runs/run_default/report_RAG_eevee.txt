Eevee (Pokémon #133) is a brown, quadruped Normal‑type that makes its home in urban environments across the Kanto region.  It can be caught with a moderate success rate of 45, belongs to the Ground egg group, and is not classified as a baby, legendary, or mythical Pokémon.  

Its genetic makeup is irregular and unstable, allowing it to adapt its body composition when its surroundings change.  Flavor texts note that “its genetic code is irregular. It may mutate if it is exposed to radiation from element STONEs,” and that “its ability to evolve into many forms allows it to adapt smoothly and perfectly to any environment.”  Because of this, Eevee can evolve into a variety of related species—vaporeon, jolteon, flareon, espeon, umbreon, leafeon, glaceon, and sylveon—depending on the stimuli it encounters.  

Eevee possesses abilities such as adaptability, anticipation, and run‑away, and it can learn a large move pool of 74 techniques, including quick‑attack, bite, tackle, take‑down, double‑edge, headbutt, body‑slam, growl, reflect, rest, sleep‑talk, substitute, sunny‑day, rain‑dance, and protect.  These traits make it a versatile and widely‑studied Pokémon in both research and battle.
