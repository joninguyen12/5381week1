Gengar, the Shadow Pokémon (ID 94), first appears in Generation I of the Kanto region. Its upright, purple form can be found most often lurking in caves, where it drifts unseen among the darkness. Gengar’s family line begins with the small Gastly, grows into the mischievous Haunter, and culminates in the fully‑formed Gengar, marking a classic three‑stage evolution.

In the night, a sudden chill is often a sign that a Gengar is near. Flavor texts describe how it steals heat from its surroundings, cloaks itself in shadows, and even mimics the silhouettes of people to startle travelers. Legends speak of it slipping into a victim’s shadow to wait for an opportunity, laughing as it casts curses beneath a full moon.

Gengar’s evolution line and its eerie habitat have made it a memorable part of Kanto’s folklore. Though it is neither legendary nor mythical, its uncanny ability to manipulate shadows and temperature earned it a reputation as one of the most feared and revered denizens of the Pokémon world.
