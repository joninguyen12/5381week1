Lapras is recorded in the Pokédex as a “Transport Pokémon” that dwells in the sea and takes the form of a gentle, blue‑colored fish.  Its most noted trait is the ability to ferry people and other Pokémon across the water on its broad back, a role it has fulfilled since the Kanto era of Generation I.  Though unusually intelligent—capable of understanding human speech—its friendly nature has made it a frequent target for hunters, driving its numbers toward near‑extinction.

Across many games, flavor texts highlight Lapras’s melancholy song, especially in the evenings when it searches for the few remaining kin.  Its gentle heart means it rarely engages in combat, and because it is easy to catch, many have been taken from the wild, further reducing its already scarce population.  The species is still listed as a common sight in coastal regions, but sightings are now far less frequent than they once were.

Evolutionarily, Lapras stands alone; there are no pre‑evolutions or post‑evolutions recorded for this species.  Its lineage remains simply “Lapras,” emphasizing its unique place as the sole member of its line.  Today, trainers who manage to encounter a Lapras often find a reliable companion for sea travel and a poignant reminder of the delicate balance between human activity and wildlife preservation in the Kanto seas.
