**Lapras** (Japanese: ラプラス) is a majestic Water‑Ice Pokémon whose sleek, whale‑like silhouette and crystal‑blue hide have made it a legend across the seas of every region. Though it is a singular species with no true evolutionary relatives, its lineage is often traced alongside other aquatic behemoths such as Articuno and Kyogre, embodying the serene power of frozen tides. First introduced in the original Kanto Pokédex, Lapras quickly earned a reputation as the “Transport Pokémon,” ferrying trainers across rivers, lakes, and even the treacherous Seafoam Islands on its broad, fin‑tipped back.

Trainers most often encounter Lapras in coastal sanctuaries and glacial waters, from the mist‑shrouded coasts of the Alola’s Seashore to the icy depths of the Galar Lake of Outrage. In the Kanto games it spawns on Route 12 and in the Seafoam Islands, while in later titles it appears as a rare gift in the Wild Area of the Crown Tundra and as a hidden encounter in the Whispering Tower of the Hisui region. Its presence is most iconic in the anime, where a single Lapras aids Ash’s journey across the Orange Islands, cementing its status as a beloved companion for explorers and sailors alike.

In battle, Lapras’s Water / Ice typing grants it impressive defensive versatility, while its abilities—*Water Absorb* (recovering HP when hit by a Water move) and its hidden ability *Rain Dish* (passively restoring health under rain)—turn it into a reliable staller and rain‑team anchor. Its signature move, **Sing**, has become synonymous with the species, putting foes to sleep and opening openings for coordinated assaults, while moves such as *Hydro Pump*, *Ice Beam*, *Thunderbolt*, and *Body Press* give it both offensive punch and strategic depth. Competitive players prize Lapras for its blend of bulk, utility, and that nostalgic, crowd‑pleasing charm that can turn the tide of any match, making it a timeless favorite in any trainer’s roster.
