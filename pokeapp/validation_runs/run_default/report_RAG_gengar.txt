Gengar is a purple, upright Shadow Pokémon with the Pokédex number 94. It belongs to the Ghost and Poison types and is part of a three‑stage evolution line that begins with Gastly, progresses to Haunter, and finishes as Gengar. In the wild it is most often found in caves.

Legends say that a sudden chill in the air often signals Gengar’s presence, because it steals heat from its surroundings and can cool the area by nearly ten degrees Fahrenheit. It hides in shadows, mimicking a person’s silhouette and even laughing at the fright it causes. On moonlit nights, moving shadows may actually be Gengar pretending to be one’s own shadow, and its eerie leer is said to belong to a Pokémon delighting in casting curses.

Gengar’s ability, Cursed Body, can hinder opponents, and it learns many moves such as Shadow Ball, Dream Eater, and Hypnosis, among others. Trainers in the Kanto region have encountered it for generations, and its uncanny habit of pretending to be a shadow has made it a memorable part of the Pokémon world.
