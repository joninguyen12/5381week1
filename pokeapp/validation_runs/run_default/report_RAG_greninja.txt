Greninja is a Water‑type / Dark‑type Pokémon that first appeared in the Kalos region of Generation VI. It is known as the “Ninja Pokémon” and evolves from Froakie through the intermediate form Frogadier before reaching its final shape. Its two abilities are Protean and Torrent, and trainers can catch it with a capture rate of 45; its habitat has not been specified in the source material.

In battle Greninja behaves like a silent shinobi, materializing water into sharp, star‑shaped projectiles that it hurls at great speed, capable of cleanly cutting through metal. Its movements are swift and elusive — it appears and disappears with ninja‑like grace, teasing opponents before delivering a decisive strike with razor‑thin water blades.

The species can learn a wide variety of moves, including water‑shuriken, shadow‑sneak, and hydro‑pump, reflecting its versatile and aggressive fighting style. Its evolutionary line — Froakie, Frogadier, and Greninja — traces a progression from a tiny seedling to a master of water‑based ninjutsu.
