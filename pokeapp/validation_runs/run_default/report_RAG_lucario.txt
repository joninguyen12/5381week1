Lucario is an “Aura Pokémon” that first appeared in the Sinnoh region.  Its body is covered in blue fur and it walks upright, wielding a combination of Fighting‑ and Steel‑type power.  This Pokémon can sense the auras of everything around it, allowing it to understand human speech and to read the thoughts and emotions of others from more than half a mile away.  Its natural abilities—inner‑focus, justified and steadfast—help it stay sharp in battle and respond to danger.

In combat Lucario commands strong aura waves that can pulverize large rocks and cut through the toughest defenses.  It often uses moves such as **aura‑sphere** and **close‑combat** to launch precise, high‑impact attacks, while techniques like **detect** and **extreme‑speed** let it anticipate opponents’ actions.  By controlling its aura, it can both read an enemy’s feelings and turn those feelings into powerful strikes, making it a feared battler that can sense even hidden threats.

Lucario evolves from the small, dog‑like Riolu and only trusts trainers who carry a strong sense of justice.  Legends say that no foe can remain invisible to it, because its aura perception reveals hidden presences and emotions alike.  Though its habitat is not specified, it is most commonly associated with the Sinnoh region, where it continues to guard and guide those who earn its respect.
