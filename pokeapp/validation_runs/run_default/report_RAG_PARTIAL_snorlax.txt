Snorlax, classified as a “Sleeping Pokémon,” is a massive, black, humanoid creature that embodies the notion of perpetual rest. Its everyday life consists almost entirely of eating and sleeping, and its enormous belly has even become a playground for children in some mountain communities. In the wild, especially within Kanto’s rugged peaks, Snorlax’s appetite knows no bounds—it can consume over eight hundred pounds of food each day before promptly collapsing into a deep slumber.

The species’ digestive system is remarkable; its stomach acids can break down even moldy or poisonous substances without harm, allowing it to subsist on food that would sicken other Pokémon. Folklore describes its rumbling cries as either snorts or the echoing hunger pangs of a belly that never seems satisfied, and the gentle thudding of its belly can be so harmless that it is safe to bounce upon once it is fully sated.

Snorlax evolves from the smaller, similarly gluttonous Munchlax, marking a progression from a modest feeder to the towering, mountain‑dwelling behemoth known across generations. Though not a legendary or mythical being, its legendary appetite and the protective nature of its immense bulk have secured Snorlax a unique place in the folklore of the Kanto region—a gentle giant whose only real threat is the sheer inconvenience of getting in its way.
