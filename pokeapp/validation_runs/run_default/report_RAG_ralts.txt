Ralts, called the Feeling Pokémon, is a small white humanoid that mainly inhabits urban areas. It rarely appears before people, but when it does it is drawn closer if it senses a positive disposition, and it will hide if it detects any hostility. Its psychic‑fairy nature lets it read the emotions of anyone nearby.  

Its abilities let it synchronize feelings with others, read minds telepathically, and copy an opponent’s effect (Trace). Ralts evolves into Kirlia and then into either Gardevoir or Gallade, giving it a brief evolutionary line. It can learn a variety of moves such as Calm Mind, Psychic and Dazzling Gleam, which showcase its psychic and fairy powers.  

Ralts is most commonly found in the Hoenn region, where its urban habitat matches the environments it prefers. With a capture rate of about 235, it is relatively easy to encounter, and trainers often meet it when they are in a cheerful mood, as the Pokémon reflects that optimism.
