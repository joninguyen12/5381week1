Mudkip is a small, blue‑colored, four‑legged Pokémon that lives on the edges of waterways.  In the Hoenn region it is known as the “Mud Fish Pokémon” and its most distinctive feature is a fin on its head that works like a radar, letting the creature sense what is happening around it without relying on sight.

In water it breathes through gills hidden behind its cheeks, and when threatened it can unleash surprising strength—crushing rocks larger than itself and even lifting sizeable boulders by planting its feet firmly in the mud.  After a bout of activity it often buries itself in the soft soil at the water’s edge to rest.

Mudkip evolves first into Marshtomp and then into Swampert, completing a three‑stage line that remains exclusive to the Hoenn region.  It possesses the abilities Damp and Torrent, and it can learn a variety of moves such as Mud‑Shot, Water‑Gun and other techniques that highlight its aquatic nature.  Though modest in size, its blend of radar‑like senses, powerful fins and stone‑lifting capability makes it a uniquely adaptable Pokémon.
