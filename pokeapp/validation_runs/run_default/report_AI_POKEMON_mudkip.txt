Mudkip is a small, blue, quadruped Pokémon that lives on land near the edge of water.  It is classified in the Pokédex as a “Mud Fish Pokémon,” belongs to the “monster” and “water 1” egg groups, and is not a baby, legendary, or mythical creature.  Its capture rate of 45 is relatively easy to obtain, and it was first introduced in the Ruby, Sapphire, and Emerald games.

The Pokédex entries from the various game versions describe Mudkip’s distinctive traits: its head fin works like a radar, letting it sense water and air movements without looking; underwater it breathes through gills on its cheeks; on land it can lift and crush boulders by planting its four feet and heaving; and it propels itself through water with a powerful tail fin.  Different releases emphasize different details — some highlight the radar‑like fin, others stress the gill breathing and rock‑crushing strength, while a few note how it rests by burying itself in mud at the riverbed.

In the evolutionary line Mudkip → Marshtomp → Swampert, the species names appear in that exact order, forming a straightforward three‑stage progression.
