Greninja is the final member of the Water‑type Froakie evolutionary line. It is classified as a “Ninja Pokémon,” has a humanoid shape, and its primary color is listed as blue. The Pokédex notes that it is neither a baby, a legendary, nor a mythical species.  

The species evolves in a straightforward sequence: Froakie → Frogadier → Greninja. Thus, Greninja represents the third and final evolution stage of that family.  

Habitat information isn’t provided for Greninja, but its egg group is recorded as “water1.” The in‑game Pokédex entries (found in versions X and Y) describe it as forming throwing‑star projectiles from compressed water and moving with ninja‑like grace, splitting metal when struck; these flavor texts differ slightly between the two versions. Greninja can be captured with a capture rate of 45.
