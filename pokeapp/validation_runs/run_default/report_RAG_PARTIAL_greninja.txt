Greninja is a sleek, blue‑hued “Ninja Pokémon” that takes on a distinctly humanoid silhouette.  Its feathers of water swirl and compress into razor‑sharp throwing stars, capable of cutting through metal when hurled at high speed.  In the wild Kalos skies it disappears and reappears with the fluid grace of a true ninja, slipping past foes while delivering cutting blows that slice with the precision of water itself.  

The creature’s lineage traces back through three forms—Froakie, Frogadier, and finally Greninja—each stage sharpening its skills as a master of both water and stealth.  Though its natural habitat is not specified, its presence in the Kalos region’s diverse ecosystems points to a preference for moist, lakeside or wetland areas where water can be gathered and reshaped at will.  

In the Pokédex, Greninja is noted for a modest capture rate of 45 and belongs to the “water 1” egg group, underscoring its classification among aquatic‑based lifeforms.  Its lore emphasizes the theatricality of its attacks: it toys with adversaries, vanishing and reappearing in a cascade of droplets, all the while mastering throwing stars fashioned from compressed water.  

The source for this snapshot comes from Generation VI’s Kalos region data, where Greninja’s vivid flavor texts highlight its ninja‑like elegance and the lethal potency of its watery projectiles.  As a non‑legendary, non‑mythical Pokémon, it remains a popular choice for trainers seeking a blend of speed, artistry, and elemental mastery.
