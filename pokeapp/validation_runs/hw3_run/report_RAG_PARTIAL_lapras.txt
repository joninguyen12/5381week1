Lapras is a gentle “Transport Pokémon” that has been driven to the brink of extinction by relentless hunting.  Its intelligence lets it understand human speech, and it takes great pride in ferrying people and other Pokémon across the sea on its back.  When it is in a good mood the creature sings a soft, plaintive cry that drifts over the water, a sound that hints at both sorrow and hope for the few survivors that still remain.

Living in the open ocean, Lapras prefers the deep sea where it can glide serenely while carrying its passengers.  Its egg groups are classified as “monster” and “water 1,” and it is found relatively commonly in the wild, though its capture rate is modest at 45.  With a blue hue and fish‑shaped body, it moves through the water with an elegance that has made it a beloved companion for travelers of the Kanto coast.

This species is native to Generation I and the Kanto region, and its evolutionary line consists of a single member—Lapras itself.  Though it is not a legendary or mythical Pokémon, its reputation as a caring, high‑intelligence caretaker of the seas has cemented its place in the folklore of coastal towns, where it is remembered both for the comfort it provides and the tragic loss it has endured.
