Snorlax, numbered 143 in the Pokédex, is classified as a “Sleeping Pokémon” with a humanoid shape and a solid black hue. Discovered in the mountains of the Kanto region during Generation I, it is a non‑baby, non‑legendary, non‑mythical creature that belongs to the Monster egg group. Its capture rate of 25 reflects a relatively rare encounter, yet it is a familiar sight in the region’s early adventures.

The creature’s daily routine revolves around an endless cycle of eating and sleeping. Snorlax can swallow anything—even moldy or rotten food—without ill effect, thanks to a stomach that produces digestive juices capable of dissolving any poison. Its rumbling belly often masquerades as a cry, and the massive bulk of its abdomen has become a playground for children who delight in bouncing on it while it remains utterly lethargic.

Snorlax’s evolutionary line begins with Munchlax and culminates in the oversized Snorlax. Both forms share the same insatiable appetite and tranquil disposition, embodying a simple yet profound design that emphasizes gluttony, rest, and the gentle, almost protective presence of a creature that lives to eat and then to slumber. This timeless image has endured across generations, remaining a staple of the Pokémon world’s lore.
