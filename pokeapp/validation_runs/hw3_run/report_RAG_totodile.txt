Totodile is a small, upright, blue Water‑type Pokémon that lives at the water’s edge in the Johto region. Known as the “Big Jaw Pokémon,” it is noted for its notably powerful bite.  

It evolves into Croconaw and later into Feraligatr. In battle it can use abilities such as Sheer Force and Torrent, and it learns moves including Bite, Aqua Jet, and Water Gun, among many others from its extensive move pool.  

Despite its modest size, its jaws can crush anything, and even its trainer must stay alert; the Pokémon will bite anything that moves, and what appears to be playful nipping can cause serious injury. Turning your back on it is therefore unwise.
