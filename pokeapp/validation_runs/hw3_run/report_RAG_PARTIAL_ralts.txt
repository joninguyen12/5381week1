In the bustling towns and quiet suburbs of the Hoenn region, a petite white‑clad humanoid drifts unnoticed, its slender horns tuned like delicate antennae to the emotional tides of those around it.  Ralts, classified as a “Feeling Pokémon,” is said to appear only where a calm or cheerful disposition lingers; when it senses hostility, it retreats quietly, preferring to linger near positive hearts rather than confronting negativity.

As it matures, this shy sentinel evolves through several familiar forms—first into the elegant Kirlia, then into either the graceful Gardevoir or the poised Gallade—each stage retaining its innate ability to read and mirror the emotions of its surroundings.  Its urban habitat makes it a common sight in bustling cities, where its presence is often marked by a subtle warming of its body when it absorbs the warm feelings of nearby people or Pokémon.

Folklore among trainers tells that a Ralts will draw nearer to anyone whose aura radiates optimism, offering a gentle companionship that uplifts the spirit.  Though rare in the wild, its occasional appearance before a hopeful trainer is considered a subtle invitation to share kindness, while its swift concealment when unease is sensed reinforces its reputation as a sensitive guardian of emotional balance.
