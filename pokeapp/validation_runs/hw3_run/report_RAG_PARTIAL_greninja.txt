Greninja, classified as a “Ninja Pokémon,” belongs to the Water 1 egg group and completes a three‑stage line that begins with Froakie, proceeds through Froggadier, and culminates in Greninja itself.  

Its lore highlights a graceful, vanishing presence reminiscent of a ninja, and it wields compressed‑water throwing stars that can cleanly split metal. These stars are described as sharp enough to slice through foes, underscoring a swift, elusive combat style.  

Originating from Generation VI in the Kalos region, Greninja is noted for a moderate capture rate of 45, reflecting its balance of rarity and accessibility within its native ecosystem.
