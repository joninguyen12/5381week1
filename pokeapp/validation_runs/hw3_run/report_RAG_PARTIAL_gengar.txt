Gengar, the Shadow Pokémon (National Pokédex No. 94), is most often found haunting caves where its presence can be felt as an unexpected chill. Its upright purple form drifts silently in the darkness, drawing heat from its surroundings and leaving an uncanny cold that can lower the ambient temperature by nearly ten degrees Fahrenheit. Trainers who sense a sudden drop while exploring dimly lit tunnels should beware, for that is the unmistakable sign of a Gengar nearby.

The lineage of this eerie creature begins with Gastly, the first stage of its evolution chain, which matures into Haunter before finally blossoming into Gengar. Each evolution deepens its spectral mastery, allowing Gengar to more adeptly blend with shadows, mimic the silhouettes of passersby, and even disguise itself as a simple outline on a wall or floor. Its capture rate of 45 reflects the elusive nature of this Pokémon—once elusive enough to slip through a full moon’s light, yet stubborn enough to be a prized addition to seasoned trainers’ rosters.

Across the many versions of its Pokédex entries, Gengar’s flavor text consistently emphasizes its playful yet predatory nature. Legends tell that under a full moon, shadows may move of their own accord and even laugh—a sure indicator that Gengar is at work, pretending to be one’s own shadow while silently awaiting an opportunity to strike. Whether lurking in the corners of abandoned homes or wandering the night‑lit paths of mountain trails, Gengar is always ready to ambush, delighting in the fear and heat it can siphon from unsuspecting victims.
