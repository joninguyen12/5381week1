**Gengar** (Japanese: ゴースト *Ghos*) is the final evolution of the Gastly line — beginning as a drifting mound of spectral gas (Gastly) that condenses into a mischievous Haunter before erupting into the fully‑formed, smirk‑wielding Gengar.  Its inky violet hue, glowing eyes, and hovering silhouette give the impression of a living ember of the night, a being that thrives on the lingering echoes of the dead.  Though it looks like a simple cartoonish specter, Gengar’s power is amplified by its Ghost/Poison typing, allowing it to poison foes while siphoning their life force with a flick of its ethereal hands.

Encountered most often in the dim recesses of abandoned towers, haunted forests, and the mist‑shrouded marshes of the Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, and Sinnoh regions, Gengar is a familiar sight to any trainer who dares to venture after dusk.  Its presence is felt as a sudden chill and a faint, echoing laugh that seems to bounce off the walls, a warning that the spirit is watching.  In the anime, it has become the iconic partner of many mischievous protagonists, while in the competitive arena it is prized for its uncanny ability to out‑maneuver opponents with swift, lingering threats.

Gengar’s defining ability, **Cursed Body**, randomly disables one of an opponent’s moves, and its hidden ability **Shadow Tag** locks foes in place, turning the battlefield into a trap of lingering dread.  Among its signature moves, **Destiny Bond** lets Gengar trade its own faint for a guaranteed KO on the opponent that manages to land a finishing blow — an elegant, if ruthless, dance of fate.  Its arsenal also includes the iconic **Shadow Ball**, the poisonous punch **Sludge Bomb**, and the hypnotic **Dream Eater**, each of which has become a staple in high‑level play.  Together these traits give Gengar the reputation of a sly, almost theatrical battler — one that can turn the tide of a match with a single, well‑timed grin.
