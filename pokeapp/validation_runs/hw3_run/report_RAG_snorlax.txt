Snorlax is a black, humanoid Normal‑type Pokémon (species ID 143) that makes its home in the mountains of the Kanto region. Though not a baby, legendary, or mythical creature, it belongs to the “monster” egg group and can evolve from the smaller Munchlax before reaching its final form.

This Pokémon is famous for its extreme laziness: a typical day consists of nothing more than eating and sleeping, and it will only stop to rest once it has devoured nearly a ton of food. Its appetite is so legendary that it can consume moldy or rotten fare without ever getting an upset stomach, and its stomach acid is strong enough to dissolve any poison.

When fully fed, Snorlax becomes so sluggish that it can be safely bounced on, and the low rumble that people hear may actually be either a snore or the deep growl of a hungry belly. Its thick‑fat and gl… abilities let it tolerate harsh conditions, and it can learn moves such as Belly‑Drum, Rest, and Snore to help it survive while it continues its endless cycle of eating and dozing.
