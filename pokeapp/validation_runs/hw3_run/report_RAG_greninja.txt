Greninja is a Water‑ and Dark‑type Pokémon that first appeared in Generation VI of the Kalos region. It is the final form of a three‑stage line that begins with Froakie and passes through Frogadier before reaching Greninja. Its two abilities, Protean and Torrent, give it the power to change its type to match the move it uses and to boost Water‑type attacks when its HP is low.

In battle Greninja behaves like a silent ninja, appearing and disappearing with graceful speed while hurling water‑shaped throwing stars. These compressed‑water projectiles spin at high velocity and can even split metal, allowing Greninja to toy with opponents and cut them down with razor‑sharp streams of liquid.

The species can learn a wide variety of moves, including Water Shuriken, Dark Pulse, Hydro Pump, and many others, giving it flexible options for both offense and support. Its capture rate is moderate, and while its habitat has not been specified, it is known to thrive in the Kalos region’s diverse environments.
