Lapras is a blue, fish‑shaped Pokémon known as a “Transport Pokémon.”  Historical records note that people have hunted it almost to extinction, yet it still commonly ferries travelers across the water on its broad back, offering a comfortable ride over the sea.

Its body is covered in water‑type and ice‑type elements, and it possesses three distinct abilities: hydration, shell‑armor, and water‑absorb.  In battle it can use a variety of moves such as hydro‑pump, ice‑beam, aqua‑tail, blizzard, and surf, among many others, allowing it to adapt to both aquatic and icy environments.

Native to the seas of the Kanto region, which introduced it in Generation I, Lapras is noted for its high intelligence and its ability to understand human speech.  When content, it may sing an enchanting cry, while in quieter moments it is said to sing plaintively, searching for the few remaining members of its kind.
