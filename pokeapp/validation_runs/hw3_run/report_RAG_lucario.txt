Lucario, identified as the “Aura Pokémon” and numbered 448 in the Pokédex, belongs to the Fighting / Steel typing and evolves from the Pokémon Riolu. Its design is upright and blue‑colored, and it is linked to the Sinnoh region of Generation IV, though its natural habitat is not specifically described.  

The species is equipped with three possible abilities—inner‑focus, justified, and steadfast—each of which influences how it reacts in battle. Lucario can perceive the auras of all living things, allowing it to sense emotions, thoughts, and even the feelings of creatures that are more than half a mile away. It is also said to be able to read human speech and understand the inner feelings of others.  

When it concentrates its mental energy, Lucario releases mysterious aura waves that are powerful enough to pulverize large rocks and can be used both to detect hidden presences and to attack opponents. Some flavor texts describe it as capable of controlling these auras in battle, while others note that it can become stressed when forced to learn too much about others’ thoughts.  

The move set available to Lucario includes techniques such as Aura Sphere, Close Combat, Steel Beam, and many others, all of which it can employ to harness its aura power and overcome a wide range of challenges.
