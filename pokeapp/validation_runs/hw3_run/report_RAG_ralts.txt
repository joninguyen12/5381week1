Ralts is a Psychic‑type and Fairy‑type Pokémon that makes its home in urban settings of the Hoenn region. It rarely shows itself to strangers, but when it does appear it is drawn to people who seem upbeat and friendly.  

This Pokémon can sense the emotions of people and other Pokémon through the horns on its head, and its own mood often mirrors that of its trainer — growing cheerful when the trainer is in a good spirits. Ralts is equipped with abilities such as synchronize, telepathy and trace, and it evolves through Kirlia into either Gardevoir or Gallade.  

In battle it can employ moves from its move pool, including psychic, dazzling‑gleam, calm‑mind and many others, using its emotion‑sensing horns to calm hostile feelings or to protect its allies. Its subtle power lets it warm slightly when it picks up warm feelings, making it a gentle companion for those it trusts.
