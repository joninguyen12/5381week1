Eevee is a brown, quadruped Pokémon that lives in urban areas. It is a Normal‑type creature with a moderate capture rate and its abilities include adaptability, anticipation, and run‑away, giving it an edge in battle.  

Its genetic code is irregular and can mutate when exposed to radiation from elemental stones, causing it to shift and evolve in many different ways. Because its DNA is unstable, it can alter its body composition to match its surroundings, allowing it to adapt smoothly to any environment. This unstable makeup means it can take on a variety of evolutionary forms depending on the conditions it encounters.  

Eevee first appeared in the Kanto region, where trainers often spot it roaming city streets. In battles it can learn moves such as quick‑attack, tackle, and double‑team, reflecting its versatile nature. Though details about its exact origins are limited to the Kanto connection, its adaptable spirit continues to charm fans worldwide.
