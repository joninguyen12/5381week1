Chikorita, the Leaf Pokémon (species ID 152), is a green, quadruped creature that lives in grassland habitats. A single leaf crowns its head and constantly releases a gentle, pleasant fragrance. That scent can sense humidity and temperature, and the Pokémon delights in basking in warm sunlight.  

It hails from the Johto region of Generation II and is the first stage of a three‑step evolutionary line that continues as Bayleef and then Meganium. In battle the Pokémon waves its leaf to keep foes at bay, while the same sweet aroma spreads a calm, friendly atmosphere around the fighters.  

Chikorita is a pure Grass‑type whose abilities include Leaf Guard and Overgrow. Its move pool—drawn from a set of 76 techniques—features moves such as Razor Leaf, Leaf Storm, Synthesis and Vine Whip, reflecting its plant‑based strengths. It remains a docile, sun‑loving companion that thrives on the subtle cues of its leafy crown.
