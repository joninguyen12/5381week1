**Snorlax, the Sleeping Pokémon** – a towering, blue‑gray behemoth whose girth rivals the very hills it slumbers atop. As the sole, fully‑evolved member of the Snorlax lineage (its only known pre‑evolution being the diminutive, friendship‑bound Munchlax), it embodies the phrase “might makes right” through sheer, unhurried presence. Two standard abilities—A​bility Immunity, which shields it from poison, and Thick Fat, which halves damage from Fire‑ and Ice‑type attacks—give it a defensive backbone, while its hidden ability, Gluttony, lets it make the most of even the smallest bites of food. In the wild, Snorlax is rarely seen awake; when it does stir, the ground trembles beneath its weight, and the very air seems to pause in reverent anticipation.

Trainers across the Pokémon world have learned to schedule their journeys around its perpetual naps. In the Kanto region, the legendary Snorlax that once blocked the entrance to the Pokémon Tower has become a cultural touchstone, while in Johto the massive “Snack‑Gate” of the Johto League often serves as a rite of passage for budding adventurers. Hoenn’s Route 115 is notorious for its snoring resident, and the Galarian mountains hide a particularly imposing specimen that locals swear can be heard from miles away. Encounters are most frequent near abundant food sources—abandoned berry orchards, deep‑cave grottos, or the plush, sun‑warmed lawns of Pokémon Centers—where the creature’s appetite for sweets is legendary.

In competitive circles, Snorlax is prized for its monstrous bulk and a movepool that turns its sluggish nature into a tactical advantage. Signature techniques such as **Curse** (to boost Attack and Defense at the cost of Speed) and **Body Press** (leveraging its high base HP to deal devastating physical damage) turn its inertia into offense. The move **Heavy Slam**—a Steel‑type attack whose power scales with its weight—adds a uniquely thematic edge, while the classic **Rest** paired with **Sleep Talk** lets it recover while still threatening foes in its slumber. With its formidable defensive stats, ability to out‑tank many threats, and a repertoire that blends raw power with strategic depth, Snorlax remains a cornerstone of any team that values durability wrapped in a deceptively tranquil package.
