Mudkip is a blue, quadruped “Mud Fish Pokémon” that lives on the water’s edge of the Hoenn region. It is a Water‑type with the abilities Damp and Torrent and can be caught with a moderate capture rate of 45.  

Its head fin works like a highly sensitive radar, letting it detect movements of water and air without relying on its eyes. In water it breathes through gills on its cheeks, and when faced with a tight battle situation it can unleash enough power to crush rocks larger than itself. On land it can lift large boulders by planting its four feet and heaving, and it rests by burying itself in mud at the bottom of a river.  

Mudkip evolves into Marshtomp and then Swampert. It learns moves such as Water‑Gun and Mud‑Shot, among many others, and its strength allows it to shift rocks and surf rivers. This species is native to the Hoenn region.
