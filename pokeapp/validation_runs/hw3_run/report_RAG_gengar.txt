Gengar, the 94th Pokémon species, is known as the “Shadow Pokémon.” It is a purple, upright creature that makes its home in caves and was first introduced in Generation I of the Kanto region. Though it is not a baby, legendary, or mythical Pokémon, its egg group is listed as “indeterminate,” and it evolves from Gastly → Haunter → Gengar. Its official typing is Ghost and Poison.  

In the wild, Gengar is said to linger in shadows, stealing heat from its surroundings and often leaving a sudden chill as a clue of its presence. It may mimic the shadows of people and laugh at their fright, waiting silently to slip into a target’s shadow and drain their life force. Its signature ability, Cursed‑Body, can prevent a foe from using moves that are already disabled, and it learns techniques such as Shadow‑Ball, Curse, Toxic, and many others that fit its mischievous, haunting nature.  

Gengar’s lore is tied to the night‑time atmosphere of the Kanto region, where a full moon or moving shadows can signal its approach. While the Pokédex notes that it can cool an area by nearly ten degrees Fahrenheit when hiding, the details of its exact movements and the breadth of its move pool are left unspecified beyond the moves listed in its data.
